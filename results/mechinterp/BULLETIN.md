## Headlines (strongest first)

1. **GD federation stays in the centralised basin; AdamW federation does not.** Setup A vs its same-init twin: relative distance 0.17 (K2) → 0.71 (K50), test accuracy at the midpoint of the straight line to the twin 100%. Transformers B and C: midpoint 5.0% (n = 32), CKA with the twin 0.48 at K2 and 1.6e-03 at K ≥ 20 (mi02, mi07).
2. **Which S5 irrep the transformer (C) uses is set by the initialisation, until federation overrides it.** Federated and same-init centralised models share the dominant irrep in 100% of runs at K ≤ 10 (three seeds, three different irreps) but 33% at K ≥ 20 (mi05).
3. **High-K transformers generalise with a collapsed unembedding.** Setup B (wd 0.1) at first crossing, K5 → K30: W_U effective rank 57.5 → 6.6 (ρ = -0.96), share of its Fourier power at DC (class prior) 0.48 → 1.00 (mi09).
4. **The S5 MLP (D) does the same in its output layer.** At first crossing, K2 → K20: W2 stable rank 44.3 → 1.7 (ρ = -0.96) while train loss at crossing rises 9.1e-06 → 0.25; no K50 run crossed (mi10).
5. **On D the circuit is ready long before the model generalises.** Circuit accuracy reaches half its rise before first crossing in 100% of 171 runs, at a median 0.44 (centralised) and 0.21 (federated) of t_first_cross (mi19).
6. **On A the delay is largely fixed at memorisation.** Sum-frequency concentration of the hidden units at t_memo vs log delay: ρ = -0.93 (n = 49); separates runs that never cross, AUC 0.98; within a condition (deviation from cell median) ρ = -0.76 (n = 47) (mi15).
7. **Memorising and generalising weights are linearly connected on MLPs, not transformers.** Test accuracy at the midpoint of memorisation → end: A 94.8% (n=97); A' 58.9% (n=12); B 2.3% (n=31); C 5.7% (n=12); D 68.6% (n=12); E 84.3% (n=17) (mi07, mi08).
8. **Many-client GD reaches the bar with units that barely combine the operands.** Setup A, α 0.25, at first crossing K20 → K97: two-operand interaction share of hidden-unit variance 0.65 → 0.10, NC1 1.04 → 15.63 (mi13).
9. **Norm moves first, Fourier sparsity follows.** Setup A: first-layer norm completes half its change before first crossing in 77% of runs; input Fourier IPR in only 7% (it sharpens after the model generalises). E: weight norm leads in 95% (mi17, mi18).
10. **Six setup-A 'starvation' runs diverged to NaN; they were not censored.** Each memorised, then blew up (at step 4,800–47,900). Their last finite global lr·λ_max (0.38–1.31) is below that of stable sibling runs (median 1.75), so global sharpness does not explain it (mi01).
11. **Client disagreement is unstructured.** Normalised pairwise cosine of client deviations ≈ −1 on every setup (independent, zero-sum); on A its correlation with log delay falls from ρ = 0.81 (raw) to 0.43 once the client count is removed (mi25).

Caveats: no same-initialisation noise floor for AdamW twin distances was measured; setup C is non-deterministic run to run; checkpoint moments are the nearest checkpoint within ×0.67–1.5 of the event; n per cell is 3–6.
